# Startup Customer Insights Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes customer purchase behavior, revenue trends, and business performance using Python, SQL, and Tableau. The dataset contains 3,900 customer records with total revenue of 233,081 and an average purchase value of 60 per transaction. The project includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), SQL-based business queries, and the development of an interactive Tableau dashboard to uncover actionable business insights.

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains customer purchase information including:

* Customer demographics
* Product categories
* Purchase amounts
* Ratings and reviews
* Subscription status
* Shipping and discount details

## Key Insights

* Total customer purchase revenue reached 233,081 across 3,900 customers.
* Clothing emerged as the top-performing category, generating over 104K in revenue.
* Accessories contributed approximately 74K in sales, making it the second-highest revenue category.
* Male customers accounted for 68% of total purchases, while female customers represented 32%.
* The average customer purchase value remained around 60 per transaction.
* Product-level analysis identified items like Blouse, Shirt, Dress, and Pants among the highest contributing products.
* The dashboard provides interactive insights into customer demographics, revenue distribution, and spending behavior for business decision-
  making.
